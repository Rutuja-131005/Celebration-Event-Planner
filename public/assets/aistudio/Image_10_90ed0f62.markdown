## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the poise and tactile intimacy of a high-end atelier studio coupled with modern computational clarity. It rejects the sterile, synthetic neon cues of generic AI platforms in favor of an **editorial, stationery-led aesthetic**—warm linen tones, bespoke typography, delicate hairline structures, and thoughtful white space.

The emotional tone balances reassurance, tasteful creativity, and systematic rigor. The interface guides the planner through high-stakes event coordination, wedding visions, and vendor logistics without overwhelming the senses, delivering intelligent automation through quiet, contextual craft rather than flashy interface gimmickry.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from fine paper stock, botanical floral arrangements, and patinated textiles. It establishes a warm, grounded light mode that prevents eye fatigue while conveying quiet luxury.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#512C3D` - Deep Plum):** Anchors high-priority actions, primary navigation bars, active states, and structural landmarks.
- **Secondary (`#B9654B` - Terracotta):** Drives critical warm focal points, prompt generation triggers, AI suggestion badges, and conversion touchpoints.
- **Tertiary (`#89977A` - Muted Sage):** Signifies confirmation, verified vendor states, RSVPs, timeline completions, and organic milestone cues. Complemented by Olive (`#626C4E`) for deeper status hierarchy.
- **Neutral Core (`#302D2A` - Charcoal):** Editorial-grade legibility for text, titles, and iconography, avoiding stark digital black. Muted metadata relies on Warm Gray (`#827B72`).
- **Canvas & Containers:** Base viewport rests on Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F0`), primary floating cards rely on Soft White (`#FFFDF9`), secondary groupings utilize Pale Sand (`#F0E8DC`), and celebration moments feature Soft Rose (`#EBD7D2`).
- **Borders & Rules:** Hairline accents utilize Champagne (`#E8D5B7`) to mimic gilded paper edges.
- **Critical / Alerts:** Form failures, budget breaches, and scheduling conflicts use Error Red (`#A63D40`).

## Typography

The typographical pairing mimics fine invitations balanced with practical planner software:

1. **Playfair Display (Headings):** Brings editorial grace, high transitional stroke contrast, and literary authority. Headings should feel set like invitation typography, celebrating the client's milestone.
2. **DM Sans (Body, Titles, Data, Labels):** Offers clean geometric proportions with humanistic warmth. It prevents visual clutter in complex vendor matrices, budgets, run-of-show schedules, and AI prompt interfaces.

Keep line lengths for editorial copy within 55–70 characters to maintain relaxed readability. Never italicize functional UI tokens or labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **structured, breathable grid** inspired by architectural folios:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas spans 1400px to avoid stretched layouts.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Complex multi-column scheduling docks collapse into tabbed panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Data grids and multi-tier prompt generators stack vertically.

Content containers balance utility and air. Generous spacing (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) sets apart unrelated event dimensions (e.g., Guest List vs. Seating Chart), while related controls stay tightly bundled using `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows and glossy blurs, opting for **low-contrast tactile outlines and warm ambient diffusion**:

- **Hairline Framing:** Surface boundaries are primarily defined by 1px solid borders in Champagne (`#E8D5B7`) or subtle surface contrast (Soft White `#FFFDF9` resting on Warm Ivory `#FAF7F0`).
- **Ambient Shadow (Stationery Float):** Elevated components (drawers, modal sheets, floating menu palettes) use a warm, deep plum-tinted diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(81, 44, 61, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(81, 44, 61, 0.04);`
- **Surface Layering:** 
  - Base: Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F0`)
  - Tier 1 (Cards, Workspaces): Soft White (`#FFFDF9`)
  - Tier 2 (Embedded Context, AI Insights, Insets): Pale Sand (`#F0E8DC`)
  - Overlays / Popovers: Soft White (`#FFFDF9`) with Champagne borders.

## Shapes

The interface utilizes a **Soft (Level 1)** shape system, mirroring high-grade paper cardstock and die-cut invitations. 

- Interactive inputs, cards, and toolbars adopt subtle 0.25rem (`rounded`) to 0.5rem (`rounded-lg`) borders.
- Overly round or bubble geometries are avoided to maintain mature, boutique elegance.
- Pill shapes are reserved solely for contextual status tags and small categorical chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Plum (`#512C3D`) surface with Soft White text, hairline border in `#512C3D`. Hover shifts to `#3E212E`.
- **Secondary / Subtle:** Soft White (`#FFFDF9`) surface, 1px solid Champagne (`#E8D5B7`) border, Charcoal (`#302D2A`) text. Hover introduces Pale Sand (`#F0E8DC`) tint.
- **AI Assist Action:** Terracotta (`#B9654B`) surface with Soft White text, paired with a small custom sparkle glyph.

### Cards & Panels
Constructed on Soft White (`#FFFDF9`) with a 1px Champagne (`#E8D5B7`) perimeter. Content is partitioned with Pale Sand inner strokes or inset groupings.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Text Fields:** Background is Soft White (`#FFFDF9`), border is 1px Champagne (`#E8D5B7`), text is Charcoal (`#302D2A`). Placeholder text sits in Warm Gray (`#827B72`). Focused states use a 1px Deep Plum (`#512C3D`) ring with zero offset glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 1px Champagne border. Selected state fills with Muted Sage (`#89977A`) or Deep Plum (`#512C3D`) with crisp white check marks.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Light background tint derived from status colors (e.g., Muted Sage for "Confirmed", Soft Rose for "RSVP Pending", Pale Sand for "Draft"), Charcoal or Plum labels, and 0.5rem or full pill radius.

### AI Prompting & Co-Pilot Canvas
- **Assistant Panels:** Set against Pale Sand (`#F0E8DC`) with Terracotta (`#B9654B`) accents. AI suggestions appear as distinct stationery "notes" with thin Champagne dividers, offering direct insertion into event itineraries.

### Moodboard & Vendor Tile
- Image tiles employ an inner 1px translucent Champagne frame with subtle hover elevation to emulate archival photo mounts.
